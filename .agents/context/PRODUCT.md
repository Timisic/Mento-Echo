# Mentor Echo Product Context

register: product

## Product purpose

Mentor Echo is a controlled research experiment platform for studying whether structured AI dialogue can support university students' professional and academic identity formation. The product must make a multi-stage experiment reliable, private, and easy to complete, not emotionally persuasive or companion-like.

## Primary users

- Participants: university students who enter with a researcher-issued anonymous participant code and complete pre-survey, AI dialogue, and post-survey stages.
- Researchers: administrators who import participant codes, monitor completion status, reset pre-surveys when justified, exclude invalid sessions, and export research data.

## Strategic principles

1. Experiment integrity over visual novelty.
2. Participants must not see assignment internals, group labels, session IDs, prompt versions, provider metadata, or admin tools.
3. Researcher actions that affect data integrity need explicit confirmation and audit reasons.
4. Chinese copy should be calm, precise, neutral, and low-interference.
5. The interface should feel like a trustworthy research platform, not therapy, counseling, diagnosis, or a social companion product.

## Product shape

Participant flow: participant code entry, experiment guide, pre-survey, AI dialogue guide, AI dialogue, post-survey, completion page.

Researcher flow: login, participant import, status dashboard, reset or exclusion with reason, sensitive export with warning.

## Anti-references

- Do not use therapy, mental-health, counseling, or companion-app language.
- Do not make the AI feel like a person, mentor avatar, or emotionally intimate assistant.
- Do not create a flashy SaaS marketing aesthetic.
- Do not expose debug traces to participants.
