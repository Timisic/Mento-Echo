# Mentor Echo Experiment Platform

Mentor Echo is an experimental AI dialogue platform for studying whether structured conversation with a general-purpose large language model can support university students' identity formation. The domain is the research experiment itself: participants move through a controlled pre-survey, group assignment, AI chat intervention, post-survey, and data export lifecycle.

## Language

### Experiment lifecycle

**Participant**:
A university student who enters the experimental platform using a researcher-issued participant code.
_Avoid_: User, personnel, account, student record

**Participant Code**:
An anonymous identifier used to connect pre-survey, group assignment or study mode, AI chat, post-survey, logs, and exported data.
_Avoid_: Name, real identity, username, self-chosen nickname

**Self-generated Participant Code**:
An anonymous participant code generated by the platform with a readable sequence plus a short anti-guessing suffix.
_Avoid_: Account registration, participant-created username, contact identity

**Experiment Session**:
The single recoverable lifecycle for one participant code from first entry through completion or exclusion.
_Avoid_: Login session, browser session, chat session

**Pre-survey**:
The questionnaire stage completed before group assignment and AI dialogue.
_Avoid_: Baseline form when referring to the platform stage

**Post-survey**:
The questionnaire stage completed after the AI dialogue is marked complete.
_Avoid_: Follow-up form when referring to the immediate platform stage

**Questionnaire Version**:
The immutable version label for the structured questionnaire definition used to interpret responses and scores.
_Avoid_: Editable survey draft

**Experiment Group**:
The condition where the AI dialogue focuses on professional/academic identity, future study or work direction, values, and identity formation.
_Avoid_: Treatment when speaking to participants

**Control Group**:
The condition where the AI dialogue stays on identity-unrelated light topics such as movies, music, food, travel, sports, and campus daily life.
_Avoid_: Blank group, no-intervention group

**Group Assignment**:
The locked decision assigning an experiment session to the experiment group or control group.
_Avoid_: Temporary route, current page choice

**Imported Assignment**:
A group assignment provided by the researcher when importing participant codes.
_Avoid_: Manual override unless the assignment changes an already-locked session

**Randomized Assignment**:
A group assignment generated by the platform on first entry when no imported assignment exists.
_Avoid_: Re-randomization

**Study One Pilot**:
The current pre-experiment flow where all participants receive the same AI dialogue condition without experiment/control group assignment.
_Avoid_: Study Two grouping, formal prompt-comparison experiment

**AI Dialogue**:
The text conversation stage between a participant and the configured large language model.
_Avoid_: Counseling, therapy, diagnosis

**Dialogue Model Provider**:
The configured source of AI responses during the participant-facing AI dialogue.
_Avoid_: Researcher agent, coding agent, admin automation

**Dialogue Model Memory**:
The provider-side conversation continuity for one experiment session's AI dialogue.
_Avoid_: Global user memory, browser-only memory, cross-participant memory

**Dialogue Completion Eligibility**:
The state reached when an AI dialogue has at least 10 participant turns and at least 15 minutes elapsed since dialogue start.
_Avoid_: AI-approved completion

**Completed Experiment**:
An experiment session with submitted pre-survey, locked group assignment, completed AI dialogue, and submitted post-survey.
_Avoid_: Finished chat

### Data and operations

**Behavior Event**:
A timestamped record of meaningful experiment activity or technical diagnostics, such as survey submission, group assignment, AI call result, resume, error, or export.
_Avoid_: Full frontend analytics, every keystroke

**Audit Log**:
A timestamped record of researcher administrator actions that change, reset, inspect, or export experiment data.
_Avoid_: Participant behavior log

**Export Package**:
A ZIP archive generated from PostgreSQL containing raw and derived data files plus a README for reproducible analysis.
_Avoid_: Database backup

**Analysis Dataset**:
A single wide CSV with one row per participant code containing completion status, group, questionnaire scores, change scores, and validity flags.
_Avoid_: Raw questionnaire response table

**Raw Chat Export**:
The sensitive export file containing complete participant and AI messages for qualitative coding or text analysis.
_Avoid_: Default analysis dataset

**Researcher Administrator**:
The single MVP backend operator who imports participant codes, monitors progress, resets stages, excludes sessions, and exports data.
_Avoid_: Multi-role staff, coder account

## Relationships

- A **Participant Code** has at most one active **Experiment Session**.
- A **Participant Code** may be imported by a **Researcher Administrator** or generated by the platform as a **Self-generated Participant Code**.
- **Self-generated Participant Codes** should use a readable sequence plus anti-guessing suffix, such as `P001-7K`, so the researcher can estimate participant count without making codes trivially guessable.
- An **Experiment Session** belongs to exactly one **Group Assignment** once assignment is determined.
- A **Group Assignment** is either an **Imported Assignment** or a **Randomized Assignment**.
- During the **Study One Pilot**, an **Experiment Session** should proceed without visible experiment/control grouping; group assignment concepts are retained for a later grouped study.
- The participant-facing experience is blinded to **Group Assignment** labels and assignment source; only the **Researcher Administrator** should see those operational details.
- A **Pre-survey** must be submitted before the **AI Dialogue** starts.
- An **AI Dialogue** uses exactly one configured **Dialogue Model Provider** for participant-facing assistant responses.
- **Dialogue Model Memory** is scoped to exactly one **Experiment Session** and must survive browser refresh or backend restart.
- A **Post-survey** must be submitted after the **AI Dialogue** is complete.
- **Dialogue Completion Eligibility** requires both 10 participant turns and 15 elapsed minutes.
- A **Completed Experiment** requires submitted **Pre-survey**, completed **AI Dialogue**, and submitted **Post-survey**.
- **Behavior Events** belong to an **Experiment Session**.
- **Audit Logs** belong to the **Researcher Administrator** action that created them.
- An **Export Package** is generated from PostgreSQL and may include a sensitive **Raw Chat Export**.
- The **Analysis Dataset** is derived data; it is not the system of record.

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** “Can we show the participant as a user and let them create an account?”
> **Domain expert:** “No. Use **Participant Code**. The platform may generate an anonymous code, but it must not store names or real identities.”
>
> **Dev:** “Should self-registration generate random-looking IDs?”
> **Domain expert:** “No. Use a sequential **Self-generated Participant Code** with a short suffix such as `P001-7K` so pilot operations can see the rough count without making codes trivial to guess.”
>
> **Dev:** “Does Study One need experiment/control group assignment now?”
> **Domain expert:** “No. The current **Study One Pilot** is single-group. Keep grouping language available for a later study, but do not make participants go through grouping now.”
>
> **Dev:** “If someone refreshes during chat, do we create a new chat session?”
> **Domain expert:** “No. Restore the same **Experiment Session** for that participant code and do not re-randomize.”
>
> **Dev:** “Can the AI decide when the dialogue is done?”
> **Domain expert:** “No. The platform determines **Dialogue Completion Eligibility** from 10 participant turns plus 15 minutes. The AI can summarize, but it does not approve completion.”
>
> **Dev:** “If we add Codex, is that for researcher-side coding automation?”
> **Domain expert:** “No. Treat Codex as a **Dialogue Model Provider** option for the participant-facing **AI Dialogue**, interchangeable with DeepSeek from the experiment flow’s perspective.”
>
> **Dev:** “Can each participant message open a fresh Codex conversation if the stored chat history is still in PostgreSQL?”
> **Domain expert:** “No. Preserve **Dialogue Model Memory** for the same **Experiment Session** so the provider continues the same conversation after refresh or restart.”

## Flagged ambiguities

- “人员” was used while discussing backend status. Resolved canonical term: **Participant** / “被试”.
- The Word questionnaire source includes “姓名” fields. Resolved MVP rule: the platform stores **Participant Code** only and does not store participant names; any name-to-code mapping is kept outside the platform by the researcher.
- “Registration” can imply creating an account or collecting contact identity. Resolved MVP rule: participant self-registration only creates a **Self-generated Participant Code** and **Experiment Session**; it does not create a personal account.
- “Session” can mean browser session, chat session, or experiment lifecycle. Resolved canonical term: **Experiment Session** for the recoverable research lifecycle.
- “Codex” can mean a coding agent or the model provider behind participant chat. Resolved MVP usage here: Codex is a **Dialogue Model Provider** for the participant-facing **AI Dialogue**, not researcher-side automation.
- “Memory” can mean global personalization, browser state, database chat history, or provider-side conversation state. Resolved MVP usage here: **Dialogue Model Memory** is scoped only to one **Experiment Session**.
- “研究一” was previously documented as a two-group experiment. Current resolved scope: **Study One Pilot** is single-group; grouped prompt-comparison logic belongs to a later study.
